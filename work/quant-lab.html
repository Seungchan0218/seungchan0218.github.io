Korea-first Quant Lab

Portfolio allocation, timing, and financial accounting.

Focus
Portfolio allocation
Tools
Python · NumPy · CVXPY
Status
Research prototype

Overview

This project explores portfolio allocation with Korea as its first market. It brings together baseline strategies, signature-based forecasts, risk-aware allocation, and an explicit accounting ledger.

The central question is how modeling choices behave when observation timing, execution delays, and transaction costs are part of the experiment.

Components

  • Equal-weight, fixed 60/40, and minimum-variance portfolio baselines.
  • Ordinary and path-signature features, evaluated under matched allocation conditions.
  • Explicit costs and accounting, including a separate four-ETF experiment with whole shares and distribution receivables.
  • Saved protocols, model parameters, source snapshots, and reproducibility records.

Evaluation principles

Forecasts only use information available at the observation cutoff. Labels must fully mature before training, and normalization is fitted on training data. Comparisons use matched evaluation conditions.

Once a saved test result has informed a modeling choice, it becomes development data. A new claim requires a clearly separated evaluation.

Scope and limitations

Execution is simulated. Synthetic demonstrations establish working calculations, not real-market profitability. This page presents the research process and does not report investment performance or trading recommendations.
